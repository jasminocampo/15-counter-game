✿ THE LITTLE FLOWER GARDEN ✿

15 Flower Game

A fun little strategy game in a blooming garden.

✿ GARDEN GUIDE ✿

How to Play

1. There are 15 flowers in the garden.

2. You can pick 1, 2, or 3 flowers per turn.

3. The computer will also pick flowers.

4. Take turns picking flowers.

5. Whoever picks the last flower wins!
